Product-type reference

Check valve

Specify the flow direction, opening behaviour and installation position.

A check valve is intended to limit reverse flow in a compatible system. Its operating behaviour depends on design and installation. Do not infer orientation or performance from the external shape, and do not treat a generic check valve as a certified backflow-prevention assembly.

01 / SPECIFY

What to specify

These are fields to confirm, not supplied model ratings. Exact values depend on the selected product and service.

Flow directionShow intended flow on the piping drawing and confirm the body's direction marking.
Opening behaviourAsk which design is offered and whether opening-pressure and flow-loss data are available.
Installation positionConfirm permitted orientation for the exact model; do not generalize from another series.
Materials and serviceIdentify body, seat and spring materials where applicable, plus medium and temperature.
System requirementsConfirm connection details, maintenance access and any required certified backflow protection separately.
02 / CHECK

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Installing against the intended flow direction.
  • Assuming every check valve permits vertical installation.
  • Using a generic check valve in place of a required backflow-prevention assembly.
03 / ASK

Questions for a manufacturer

  1. Which installation orientations are permitted for this model?
  2. What opening-pressure and flow-loss data apply to our operating range?
  3. How is service direction marked and checked before installation?
  4. What inspection or maintenance procedure is specified?

Do not confuse it with…

Check valves address reverse-flow behaviour. Isolation valves and certified backflow assemblies have different purposes; compare against the actual system requirement.
