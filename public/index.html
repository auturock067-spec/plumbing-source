Buying guides that save research time

Short checklists covering technical questions and common sourcing mistakes.

Buying guide

BSP vs NPT: what to check

Nominally similar threaded fittings can differ in profile and sealing approach.

  • Confirm the exact thread standard and size on drawings.
  • Ask for dimensions or gauges—not a product photo alone.
  • Specify sealing method, fluid type and local code requirements.
Buying guide

How to write a clearer RFQ

Incomplete specifications cause wrong quotations and costly substitutions.

  • List quantity, drawing and end-use conditions.
  • State test reports, material certificates and packaging requirements.
  • Ask for sample policy, lead time, trade terms and exclusions.
Buying guide

Checking a valve quotation

Price comparisons only matter when the technical basis matches.

  • Confirm material grades and genuine test documents.
  • Compare pressure/temperature envelopes and product standards.
  • Clarify inspection scope, spare parts and warranty terms.

Frequently asked questions

Buy with more confidence

Answers explain what this information can and cannot tell you.

Does this site sell fittings?

No. This is an educational reference prototype; there is no checkout or live supplier quotation service.

Can two similar-looking fittings use the same threads?

Not necessarily. Thread geometry and applicable sealing methods can differ. Confirm by standard and drawing rather than appearance.

Are the manufacturers verified?

No manufacturer profiles have been published. Future listings will distinguish public records, supplier declarations and independently reviewed documents.
